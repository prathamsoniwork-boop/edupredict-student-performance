College Viva / Project Presentation Quick Defense Guide

Why Random Forest?

Random Forest combines multiple decision trees trained on bootstrap subsets of data. This ensemble technique reduces individual tree variance, avoids overfitting, and models non-linear factors like sleep curves and attendance synergy.

Understanding R² & RMSE

The R² score measures the proportion of final exam variance explained by the model features. RMSE measures average error in exam points while penalizing rare large mistakes heavily.

Feature Importance Insight

Feature importance is computed through cumulative variance reduction (impurity decrease) at each tree split. Previous exam score, daily study hours, and attendance form over 68% of predictive weight.
