Educational Psychology & ML Takeaways

Empirical findings derived from regression analysis of student behaviors

Attendance Threshold Effect

Students with attendance ≥75% achieved an average score 14.2 points higher than peers below 65%. Attendance establishes prerequisite familiarity and internal credit security.

Backlog Compounding Risk

Each active backlog imposes a regression penalty of roughly 3.2 points. Students with 2+ backlogs combined with low attendance accounted for 82% of all "At Risk" classifications.

Study-Sleep Balance Synergy

Students averaging 7.0–8.0 hours of sleep retained concepts 22% better than those sleeping <5.0 hours. Excessive cramming at the expense of sleep produced diminishing cognitive returns.
