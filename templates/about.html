About EduPredict System

Design philosophy, Machine Learning pipeline architecture, and project technical overview.

The Problem

Students frequently struggle academically due to an interwoven set of factors including declining attendance, irregular study habits, prior academic backlogs, poor sleep schedules, and limited learning resources.

In traditional university systems, academic difficulties are often detected only after midterm or semester-end exam results are declared, leaving little or no time for timely remedial intervention.

The Solution

EduPredict harnesses Machine Learning algorithms (Random Forest Regressor) to analyze both academic markers and behavioral indicators in real time, estimating expected performance before exams occur.

The platform categorizes students into tiered support levels (Excellent, Good, Average, At Risk) and automatically generates tailored, non-judgmental recommendations to guide self-improvement and academic counseling.

Instructor Presentation & Viva Tips

When demonstrating this project to a panel or professor, structure your explanation in 4 concise steps:

  1. 1. Explain the Motivation: State that traditional education is reactive; EduPredict makes academic support proactive through predictive machine learning.
  2. 2. Walk Through the ML Pipeline: Show the dataset (1,500 records), explain how categorical variables like parental support and device access were encoded, and why the 80/20 train-test split was used.
  3. 3. Justify Model Selection: Show the Model Performance page comparing Linear Regression, Decision Tree, and Random Forest across MAE, RMSE, and R². Highlight that Random Forest was chosen because it models non-linear interactions (e.g. sleep curve and attendance synergy) and provides feature importances.
  4. 4. Demonstrate Live Prediction: Use the "Recommended Demo" button to show live prediction, the circular score gauge, and the automated personalized recommendations.
